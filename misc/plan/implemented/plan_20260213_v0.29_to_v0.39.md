v0.39.0 Structural Simplification Plan: Detailed Design  
  
Summary of the Diagnosis  
  
The v0.38.0 mentor_generator.json (290 lines) has accumulated complexity through five rounds of targeted fixes (v0.35.0  
through v0.38.0). Each fix solved a specific behavioral bug, but collectively they transformed the file from v0.29.1's  
simple linear script into a 4-phase FSM with heartbeat tags, LOCKED/IN_PROGRESS statuses, and a split-path architecture  
(interactive_input_sequence for collection, post_collection_protocol for post-collection steps). This FSM architecture  
works well on Claude and GPT-class models but fails on Qwen and causes inconsistency on Gemini because:  
  
1. The broken linear path forces weaker models to discover and follow a cross-object jump from the end of  
interactive_input_sequence to post_collection_protocol.  
2. The FSM machinery (state_tracker, procedure_control_flow with LOCKED/IN_PROGRESS, heartbeat tags) reads as a  
configuration to be described rather than a prompt to be executed.  
3. Excessive meta-instructions (11 CAPITALIZED rules in _notes) compete with content for attention budget.  
4. Object-wrapped questions ({ "ask": "..." }) add structural noise compared to bare strings.  
  
Design Principle  
  
Restore v0.29.1's execution model (one linear sequence, minimal meta-instructions, simple control flow) while retaining  
v0.38.0's content and feature improvements (3-file output, external templates, course_history, FILE_AS_ANSWER, persona  
confirmation, rich guidance text).

Risk Assessment  
  
Risk 1: Bare-string Q2-Q9 may reintroduce the content-confusion bug (v0.37.x RC2).  
- Mitigation: The original bug was caused by mixed formats (greeting was bare string, Q1 was array, Q2-Q8 were bare  
strings, Q9 was bare string). v0.39.0 has a consistent format: greeting and Q1 are objects (because they have multi-field  
behavior), Q2-Q9 are ALL bare strings. The format-switching that confused the AI (object -> string -> object) is  
eliminated.  
- Fallback: If the bug reappears, normalize Q2-Q9 to {ask: "..."} as a targeted patch (Change 3b).  
  
Risk 2: Without state_tracker, the model may skip questions on strong-context inputs.  
- Mitigation: The _notes.sequence rule ("you MUST still ask every question in order") and  
persona_mapping_protocol.pre_condition both prevent this. v0.29.1 proved these are sufficient.  
- Fallback: Add question_9.critical_instruction back as a targeted patch if Q9 skipping reappears.  
  
Risk 3: Restoring the linear path may enable the "short-circuit" problem (jump from Q8 to generation).  
- Mitigation: The linear path actually reduces short-circuiting risk. In v0.38.0, the model could "see"  
post_collection_protocol at the same indent level as meta_prompt_logic and jump to it. In v0.39.0,  
validation/guidance/generation are items 5-7 in a 7-item sequence -- the model must traverse items 3 (collection) and 4  
(mapping) before reaching them. The structural distance is larger in v0.39.0 than in v0.38.0.
